Introduction

Peptide Dose ("we", "the app") is operated by Sharon Lai, Sydney, NSW, Australia. This policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile app on iOS.

Important

The app is for personal organisation and research-related logging only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. You must be 18 or older to use the app.

1. Information the App Processes

Information you enter

Information you choose to save in the app may include: profile details (such as name or email if you provide them), peptide protocols, dose logs, reminder preferences, check-ins (such as mood, energy, weight, notes), and related notes. Some of this may be considered health-related depending on how you use the app.

On-device storage

Much of this information is stored on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. It remains on your device unless you use cloud features described below.

Account and cloud sync (optional)

If you create an account or enable features that sync data, information may be sent to our backend provider (Supabase) to authenticate you and store your data so it can sync across devices. What is stored matches what you save in the app. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256).

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscription status are processed by Apple and by our subscription provider (RevenueCat) to validate entitlements and restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment card number from the app.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. We do not access your messages or contacts for this purpose.

Exports

If you use export features, data is packaged on your device and shared using the system share sheet (e.g. Mail, Files). Where the file goes after that is under your control.

Technical and diagnostic data

The app and our service providers may process limited technical information needed to run the app, such as device type, app version, and error diagnostics. We do not use third-party crash analytics in the app today.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above to provide the app's features — logging, reminders, cloud sync if enabled, subscription access, and support. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with pharmaceutical companies.

3. Sharing

  • Supabase (account and cloud sync, if you use it) — supabase.com/privacy

We may also share information if required by law.

  • Subscription records are retained as needed for billing and fraud prevention per Apple and RevenueCat policies

5. Your Choices

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a protocol?

Tap "+ Add Protocol" on the Today screen. Follow the 3-step setup — select your peptide, set your dose and frequency, and optionally add vial details for the reconstitution calculator.

How does the reconstitution calculator work?

Enter your vial size in mg and the volume of bacteriostatic water in mL. Then enter your target dose. The calculator instantly shows the exact draw volume in mL and insulin syringe units (IU).

What does the serum level bar show?

An estimated percentage of the peptide still active in your system based on its half-life and the time since your last logged dose. For organisational reference only — not a medical measurement.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Profile → tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete My Account". All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Or email support@peptidedose.app and we will process it manually.

Is my data private?

Yes. Data is stored locally on your device by default. If you create an account it is stored using encrypted cloud storage. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
