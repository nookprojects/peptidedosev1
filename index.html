Overview

Peptide Dose ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

Who We Are

Peptide Dose is developed and operated by Sharon Lai, Sydney, NSW, Australia.
Contact: privacy@peptidedose.app

1. What Data We Collect

Data you provide directly:

  • Name and email address (if you create an account)
  • Biological sex, age, and body weight (entered during onboarding for dose range personalisation)
  • Peptide protocol details including peptide name, dose, frequency, and injection site
  • Dose logs including date, time, and injection site used
  • Vial inventory details
  • Daily check-in data including mood, energy levels, weight, and personal notes

Data collected automatically:

  • App usage data (screens visited, features used)
  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash reports and error logs

What we do NOT collect:

  • Payment information (handled entirely by Apple)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or camera access (except for optional photo features)
  • Health app data without explicit permission

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to:

  • Provide and improve the Peptide Dose app and its features
  • Personalise dose range recommendations based on your biometrics
  • Sync your protocols across your devices (if you create an account)
  • Send account-related emails such as email confirmation and password reset
  • Analyse app performance and fix bugs

We do NOT use your data to:

  • Sell to third parties — ever
  • Target you with advertising
  • Share with pharmaceutical companies
  • Build profiles for marketing purposes

3. Data Storage

Local storage: Your protocol and dose data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted unless you create an account.

Cloud storage (if account created): If you choose to create an account, your data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant cloud database provider. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Your data is stored on servers located in Australia and the United States.

4. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: all personal data permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion request
  • Local data: remains on your device until you uninstall the app

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data in JSON or CSV format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights contact: privacy@peptidedose.app. We will respond within 30 days.

Peptide Dose is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information please contact us immediately at privacy@peptidedose.app and we will delete it promptly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a protocol?

Tap "+ Add Protocol" on the Today screen. Follow the 3-step setup — select your peptide, set your dose and frequency, and optionally add vial details for the reconstitution calculator.

How does the reconstitution calculator work?

Enter your vial size in mg and the volume of bacteriostatic water in mL. Then enter your target dose. The calculator instantly shows the exact draw volume in mL and insulin syringe units (IU).

What does the serum level bar show?

An estimated percentage of the peptide still active in your system based on its half-life and the time since your last logged dose. For organisational reference only — not a medical measurement.

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Profile → tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used to make the original purchase.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Profile → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete My Account". All data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Or email support@peptidedose.app and we will process it manually.

Is my data private?

Yes. Data is stored locally on your device by default. If you create an account it is stored using encrypted cloud storage. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
